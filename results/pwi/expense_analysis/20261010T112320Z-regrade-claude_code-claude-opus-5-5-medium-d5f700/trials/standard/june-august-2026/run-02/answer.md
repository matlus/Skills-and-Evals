- **August was much higher than June or July.** Without the trip, August spending was $4,082, which is 25% more than June and 41% more than July. Nearly every category went up, not just travel.

The extra visits were mostly repeat places: Luigi's Pizza (3 visits), Sushi Kame (3), Chipotle (2), McDonald's (2) and Rustic Tap.

1. **Your dining budget is the one actually being broken, not shopping.** Shopping stayed $604 under its limit for the quarter, and August went over by only $18.

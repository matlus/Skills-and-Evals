## The three most important things to know

1. **Dining went well over budget in August, and the rise was sudden.** You spent $399 against $260, which is 53% over. June and July were under budget. The average visit stayed around $22, but the number of visits doubled from 9–10 to 18. Almost all of these were local places, not trip meals, so eating out more often caused the overspend.

2. **August cost much more even without the trip.** The trip cost $1,632. Without it, August was still about $4,014: 23% more than June and 39% more than July. Groceries, gas, dining and shopping all rose in the same month, so this may be a new normal rather than a one-off.

3. **Shopping reached your $1,200 limit in August, mainly because of electronics and books.** Best Buy, Barnes & Noble and Old Navy did more to push it up than Amazon. The quarter as a whole is fine at about $1,000 a month, but August is the first month it reached the line.
